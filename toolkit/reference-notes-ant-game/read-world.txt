# World art spec: "brick-built garden" look (from 17 screenshots)

Sizes are in Roblox studs; a character is about 5 studs tall and 2 wide at the torso. Sizes of large or distant things are estimates. One prop is a real-world branded drinks cup and is deliberately left out.

## 1. Building-brick look

- **Stud pattern on everything.** Ground, paths, cliffs, tree trunk, fruit, stalls and signs all show a grid of raised square bumps with slightly rounded corners (not the classic round stud).
- **Stud size.** Pitch is about 1 stud, so a character is about 5 bumps tall and its torso covers 2. Each bump is about half the pitch (0.5 stud).
- **Stud shading.** The bump is the same colour as the surface. It is drawn only by a thin lighter line on its top/left edges and a line about 12–15% darker on its bottom/right. On the ground it looks like a texture, not geometry (guess).
- **Side faces.** Vertical faces show thin horizontal brick seams about 0.4–0.5 stud apart (seen on the giant fruit and trunk). Cliffs carry the same stud grid on their vertical faces, which reads as fine dots at distance.
- **Hero props use chunkier nubs.** Stall pillars and beams have real-looking square nubs about 0.8 stud across, sticking out about 0.2 stud, on a 1.5–2 stud pitch.
- **Colour.** One flat, saturated colour per part: no gradients, noise or gloss. Shading comes only from the sun, with side faces about 15–25% darker than tops.
- **Voxel shapes.** Nothing is round. Curves are stair-stepped in 1-block increments (fruit corners, arches, tree branches at 45°, cliff edges). Thin things are square sticks.

## 4. Living things

  - Stepped pyramid of red-brown dirt (150,85,55). The clearest one has three tiers with 3, 2 and 1 doorways.

## Rebuild recipe for the bee game

- **Studs.** Make one original square-stud tile and apply it as a tiling Texture (1 stud per tile, mostly transparent) over flat-coloured SmoothPlastic parts. Use real nub parts only on the hive and stalls.
- **Ground.** Keep the greens, orange path and yellow plaza unchanged; they already read as honey colours. Generate the stepped-edge plates from a small grid in a script. Add the green kerbs and brown stall borders.
- **Flower Garden (replaces tree and fruit).** One giant block flower or blossom tree in the middle on the pale lime plaza, inside the same fence. Giant flower heads 5–8 studs across (pink, white, purple, yellow centres) replace the fruit. States: full petals → half → bare centre.
- **Beehive (replaces Ants Nest).** Same stepped tiers in honey gold (245,185,50) and amber (220,140,30) with dark brown caps (90,55,30). One stepped-arch cell per working bee; the frame colour shows rarity.
- **Bees (replace ants).** Yellow and black striped boxes, two pale translucent wing plates, stick antennae, 2–3 studs long. They hover and bob, carrying a small honey jar with a yellow number above.
- **Honey Market (replaces Sell Food).** Yellow/white striped awning, amber counter, and a jar-shaped sign showing the amount. Keep the gold label, multiplier bar and refresh timer.
- **Queen Bee pad (replaces RollAnt).** Tan pad with a white label and orange outline reading "Queen Bee".
- **Upgrade Shop.** The sand-coloured studded pavilion with the yellow striped roof, lanterns and a row of hanging yellow blocks.
- **Scenery.** Keep the fences, tufts, block flowers, lanterns, string lights and the lilac cliffs with green caps. Purple-grey sets off the yellow well.
- **Hive bubbles.** A BillboardGui that shows through scenery with a round headshot (the one place an image is needed: the player's own thumbnail), "<Name>'s Hive" above and a jar icon with a green "/s" rate below. Add a honey-yellow beam for your own hive and pale blue for others.
- **Locked things.** Wrap them in dark block-chain loops.
- **Not copied.** The branded cup, the truck, and the other game's names and signs.

## Biggest change for least work

1. Stud texture plus flat saturated colours on the existing ground and buildings.
2. Scripted ground patchwork: three greens, orange path and yellow plaza with stepped edges and kerbs.
3. Lighting: bright sun, soft shadows, blue haze, slightly raised saturation, glow on lamps.
4. Gold outlined floating labels over the Honey Market, Upgrade Shop, Queen Bee pad and Flower Garden.
5. A script that scatters grass tufts and block flowers, plus a fence ring round the garden.
6. A backdrop ring of lilac cliff boxes with green caps.
7. The tiered hive with coloured arch cells, and hive bubbles with beams.
