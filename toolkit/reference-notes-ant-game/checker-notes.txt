# Checker notes

## The ten traits that most make the game look the way it does
1. Put every piece of 2D text straight on the world with no plate: white or one bright colour, heavy rounded font, near-black stroke of about 2-3 px at 800 px tall plus a 1-2 px downward shadow.
2. Build the whole world from flat, single-colour, saturated blocks with a square stud grid on every face and stair-stepped edges instead of curves or diagonals; no gradients or surface textures other than the studs.
3. Make every window the same kit at about 47% of screen width: black body at 50-60% opacity with no screen dimming, about 3 px dark outline, a coloured header split hard into a lighter top half and deeper bottom half, a tilted sticker icon overhanging the top-left, a white outlined title and a chunky red square X at the top-right.
4. Give every button a dark 2 px outline, small corner radius, a pale top fading into a saturated body and a darker bottom lip of about 10-15% of its height, with one fixed colour per meaning (yellow buy, green confirm/on, red remove, grey disabled, blue neutral, magenta/rainbow premium).
5. Use one rarity colour set everywhere at once: name text, card gradient, doorway arch and sparkle colour all switch together (Common grey-white, Uncommon green, Rare cyan-blue, Epic purple, Legendary gold-orange, Mythic red, Secret rainbow).
6. Keep the HUD plate-less and at the edges: three small coloured buttons at the top centre with the middle one about 1.3x bigger, a left column of sticker icons over one-word labels, tiny bare icons top-right, lime cash text bottom-left, and hide all of it (except the hotbar) while a window is open.
7. Lay the ground as a patchwork of vivid plates (three greens, orange path, yellow plaza) whose borders are rectangular pixel steps, ringed by lilac-grey terraced cliffs with green caps under a bright blue hazy sky.
8. Make the player's base a stepped pyramid of dirt tiers with grass caps (about 4 studs per tier, 4.5 per slot, one slot fewer each tier), each slot a stepped arch tinted by its occupant's rarity, with a gold hexagonal padlock when locked and green 'Empty' when free.
9. Mark stations with big gold-orange outlined billboard titles and game-show furniture: navy screens framed by alternating red and glowing yellow blocks, a flat lime price pill, a cyan 'New!!' tag tilted about 35 degrees, and a red-to-green multiplier bar with a yellow triangle and a red countdown number.
10. Show state loudly: red '!' on HUD icons and red dots on tabs/tiles for claimable things, countdowns on everything timed, black silhouettes with '???' for undiscovered, big lime ticks with 'Claimed', grey price pills when unaffordable, and rainbow fills only for paid or top-tier items.

## Details the specs missed
- [Text treatment (all 2D text)] Besides the dark stroke, big text has a small downward drop shadow: the black under the glyphs is about twice as thick as above them (about 2 px stroke + 1-2 px shadow at 800 px tall). Clear on 'Welcome Back!', window titles, 'Keep 1x', 'Restocks in:'. Build as UIStroke plus a dark copy offset 1-2 px down, or a thicker stroke nudged down.
- [Icons] Every HUD and header icon is a glossy sticker-style picture with its own 1-2 px dark contour (book, basket, stopwatch, calendar, gear, backpack, cash bundle), which is why they read with no plate behind them. Header icons are a bit taller than the header and hang over its top-left corner.
- [HUD top-centre row: hover/press state] In one frame 'Ants Nest' is about 1.22x larger (16.4% of screen width instead of 13.4%), with a thicker black outline (about 3 px) and a flatter, slightly darker fill, and the two side buttons are pushed outward by about 1.3% of screen width. So buttons scale up on hover/press and the row re-flows (list layout), rather than overlapping.
- [Layering] Draw order is world billboards < in-world popup < HUD < windows. World labels are not hidden where they collide with the HUD: the carried creature's name sits behind the top row and the event timer covers its 'LV.1' line; the 'Sell Food' block sits under 'X2 Cash'. World labels also show through the translucent window body (green 'Empty' through Playtime Rewards). Keep the bee game's top-centre strip clear of tall billboards or it will look the same.
- [Windows: scrolling and shadows] No scrollbar is drawn anywhere. Scrollable lists are simply cut off mid-row at the window's bottom edge (third Items Shop row, fifth Index row, sixth Settings row), which is the only scroll hint. Windows and buttons have no drop shadow or glow; separation from the world is only the about 3 px dark outline plus the translucent body.
- [Bottom-right visit timer] It is a second read-out of the same event as the top-centre text: values are identical in every frame ('Ends in : 3:51' with '03 : 51', 2:35 with '02 : 35', 0:52 with '52'). In the world the same event is a blue truck with a billboard 'Food Buyer's Visit' (lime) and 'Price: 2.5x !' (yellow). The tiny label under the icon is teal on two lines ('Food Buyer's' / 'Visit').
- [Body-text weight] Secondary text is a lighter weight than the titles: settings row labels, shop descriptions and the event timer use a medium-weight rounded face with only a thin (about 1 px) stroke, while titles and buttons use the heavy weight with a thick stroke. Shop item names have a vertical gradient (lighter top, deeper bottom) and a thin stroke; 'BUY' is pale yellow-white with an olive outline.
- [Price pills: two different builds] Stall pills are flat pure lime (about (1,254,3)) with an about 2 px black outline and corner radius about 20% of height. Upgrade-board pills are a smooth vertical gradient, pale mint (203,240,203) at top to green (60,232,58) at the bottom, with a dark-green outline; the unaffordable version is lavender-white with a dark-grey outline. Both use white text with a thick black stroke.
- [Offer stall small parts] The four frame corners are red blocks with one round yellow stud in the middle; the offered creature stands on a thin pale plate on the green cap; the 'Common' rarity word is a white-to-grey vertical gradient and is larger than the name line; the stall prompt fades in semi-transparent as you approach.
- [Upgrade board panels] Title, value text and sometimes the pill are each rotated a few degrees (about 3-5 degrees up to the right) independently, which gives a hand-placed look; icons carry a dark outline; gradients run diagonally (top-left to bottom-right) on the pink and cyan panels and vertically on the peach-red one.
- [Daily Rewards cards] Card backgrounds carry a faint lighter diagonal sheen; the 'bright band along the bottom' is the frame colour showing as two small blocks either side of the grey Day pill; 'Legendary' is a yellow-to-orange gradient; the Day 7 card has a magenta outline; quantity text overlaps the picture's lower-right corner.
- [Queen level] The Settings row 'Current Queen Ant Level' shows a small picture of the blue ice queen instead of a toggle, which supports the idea that the queen has levels/skins tied to progress (the queen changed from the striped white/black version at 1x luck to the ice version by 9x luck). The first queen's abdomen is white, lilac and grey-taupe bands; the black is the head and thorax.
- [Selection highlight] The white outline on the selected slot is drawn through objects in front of it (it is visible across the avatar's torso), i.e. an always-on-top highlight, not a surface outline.
- [Jelly timer look] The timer above a slot is a flat 2D sticker (jelly on a grey plate, dark contour) with a dark rounded capsule overlapping its lower third and white mm:ss text; it faces the camera and sits about half a stud above the arch top.
- [Ant info popup buttons and text] '$30' is green text with a dark-green stroke on a flat yellow button; 'REMOVE' is small white caps on flat red; both have a black outline and about 6 px corner radius. 'Uncommon' in the popup has almost no stroke, unlike the title above it.
- [World props not described] A fairground corner sets much of the mood: a giant red/white striped popcorn tub with a cream popcorn top, strings of warm bulbs and bunting; also a purple ring portal with a sparkle and small label for the Starfall event, and a 'Trash' station (yellow title, red crate with a yellow X). None are needed, but the giant-scale novelty props are part of the look.
