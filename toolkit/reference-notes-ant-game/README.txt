# Build An Ant Empire: what I noted while you played

Taken from about 50 screenshots of your game window on 3 Oct 2026. I only watched; I never clicked anything in your game. Nothing here is copied art: these are notes on the *style*, which I rebuilt with our own bee-themed pieces.

The detailed notes are in three files next to this one, plus one checker file:

- [read-stations.md](read-stations.md): the roll station, upgrade boards, sell stand and the nest
- [read-world.md](read-world.md): the ground, scenery, creatures and floating labels

## The ten things that make it look the way it does

1. **Text sits straight on the world.** White or one bright colour, a heavy rounded font, a dark outline, no box behind it.
2. **Everything is toy bricks.** Flat, bright, single-colour blocks with square studs on every face and stepped edges instead of curves.
3. **Every window is the same kit.** A dark see-through body, a coloured title bar with a tilted picture, a white outlined title and a chunky red X. The game is not dimmed behind it.
4. **Buttons have fixed meanings.** Yellow = buy, green = confirm, red = remove, grey = can't, blue = neutral, magenta or rainbow = paid. Each has a dark outline and a darker bottom lip.
5. **One rarity colour set, used everywhere at once.** Common grey-white, Uncommon green, Rare blue, Epic purple, Legendary gold, Mythic red, Secret rainbow.
6. **The HUD hugs the edges.** Three small buttons top-centre (middle one bigger), a column of picture buttons on the left, lime-green cash bottom-left. All of it hides while a window is open.
7. **The ground is a patchwork.** Three greens, orange paths and yellow plazas with square-stepped borders, ringed by lilac cliffs with green tops under a hazy blue sky.
8. **The player's base is a stepped pyramid.** One arched doorway per ant, tinted by rarity, with a gold padlock when locked and a green "Empty" when free.
9. **Stations look like a game show.** Big gold outlined titles, dark screens framed by red and glowing yellow blocks, lime price pills, a tilted cyan "New!!" tag, a red-to-green price bar with a countdown.
10. **State is shown loudly.** Red "!" on things you can claim, countdowns on everything timed, black silhouettes with "???" for undiscovered, grey price pills when you can't afford something.

## What I have built into Build A Bee Empire so far

- Points 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 are done across the whole game.
- Point 9 is partly done: gold station titles, the odds screen with the red and yellow frame, the upgrade board with one bright panel per upgrade and green or grey price pills.
- Point 5: our three bee rarities keep their own colours for now (yellow, green, blue), because the bee's body uses the same colour.

## What I saw but have not built (each one is a new feature)

- A Honey Market price that changes every few seconds
- Index, Daily Rewards, Playtime Rewards, Settings and Codes, offline earnings
- A picture bubble and light beam over each player's home
- Mounts, the item shop with potions, timed events

## What I could not see

- Animation speed and sounds cannot be judged from still pictures.
